Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

Louis Post Studio is a personal tool operated by Louis (github.com/ldumont1007). Its only user is its owner.

2. Data we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (TikTok user ID, display name and avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, and permission to upload drafts through the video.upload scope.

3. How data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's own videos and photos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub settings and are never published. No database of users is kept. Media files are kept only as long as needed to upload them.

5. Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, through its official API.

6. Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions. To ask about or delete any data, contact the owner at github.com/ldumont1007.
