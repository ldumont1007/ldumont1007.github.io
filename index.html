Louis Post Studio

Louis Post Studio is a personal tool used by its owner, Louis, to send videos and photo posts he creates to his own TikTok accounts as drafts.

How it works

  1. Louis signs in with TikTok once and approves access to upload drafts.
  2. Finished videos or photo posts are sent to the TikTok inbox of his own account through TikTok's Content Posting API (upload mode).
  3. Louis opens each draft in the TikTok app, reviews it, adds a sound and caption, chooses who can see it, and publishes it himself.

Nothing is ever published automatically. The tool does not read other users' data, does not post on anyone else's behalf, and is not offered to the public.
